# Same-order evaluation yardstick

The user approved establishing a fair assessment of material utilization before further restricted-prefix mechanisms. Keep the research topic and all existing algorithms unchanged. Add evaluation-only whole-order multistart and whole-sequence simulated annealing (SA), sharing the staged method's decoder, geometry and adaptive decoder profile. These are reproducible reference baselines, not claimed SOTA implementations.

Both start with the canonical complete Greedy layout. Try six deterministic alternatives (long edge, short edge, perimeter, aspect, width, height), retaining the best completed solution. Multistart then uses area-biased randomized whole-order rankings. SA instead uses equiprobable whole-sequence swap, relocation, reversal and block relocation; its energy is minus panel count plus 0.01 times mean squared envelope utilization, temperature 0.25 cooled by 0.995 per completed SA proposal, floored at 0.01. Keep the best complete incumbent even when accepting a worse current state. Maximum optional proposals: 6000, never a prefix restriction. Reuse unchanged-prefix snapshots without changing decoding. Preserve legacy baseline_sa.

Interrupted optional trials retain the saved complete incumbent; an interrupted initial Greedy propagates failure. Return the saved snapshot without a final replay. Record attempted/completed/interrupted evaluations and initial/best panels, decoder mode and elapsed improvement events.
